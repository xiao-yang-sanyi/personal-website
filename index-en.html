About

About Me

Ph.D. Candidate · Department of Construction Management & Intelligence, PolyU

Turning heterogeneous data into actionable quality intelligence is the central question of my doctoral research.

I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Construction Management & Intelligence (CMI) at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University (Supervisor: Dr. Hung‑Lin Chi; Co‑Supervisor: Prof. Heng Li), with a thesis titled “Intelligent Quality Management for Prefabricated Construction Based on Multimodal Data and Hybrid Intelligence: From Assessment, Prediction to Inspection.”

My research delivers an end-to-end intelligent quality-management framework built on three interconnected modules: ontology-driven multimodal quality assessment (16 quality influence factors + BP‑AdaBoost), TQM-grounded quality prediction (a dual-stream BP‑LSTM network), and robotics-enabled intelligent inspection (a Unitree B2 quadruped robot dog with an adaptive feature-fusion enhanced YOLOv8).

I aim to bring computer vision, deep learning, knowledge engineering (ontology & semantic web) and robotics onto prefabrication sites — upgrading quality management from manual, experience-based post-hoc inspection to explainable, real-time proactive intelligence.
